# Eveoy pricing

## Per customer

**$24.99 per verified customer.** That price includes the full eight-outcome bundle: visit, photos/video, content rights, CRM data, done-for-you service, and the auto-refund guarantee.

## Entry pilot

**$999 for 40+ verified customers.** The lowest-friction way to try Eveoy. No contract, no minimum term, cancel anytime. Auto-refund on any non-compliance.

## How spend scales

| Spend     | Verified customers | Use case                                     |
|-----------|--------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| $999      | 40+                | Entry pilot — public floor                   |
| ~$2,499   | 100                | Single-store proof window (~2 weeks)         |
| ~$9,996   | 400                | Multi-store / multi-week pilot               |
| ~$24,990  | 1,000              | Regional rollout test                        |
| $100K+    | 4,000+             | Full GTM activation (custom quote)           |

Pricing is linear at $24.99/customer above the pilot floor.

## The guarantee never changes

Auto-refund on non-compliance applies at every spend tier. If a visit doesn't clear verification (right customer, 15+ minutes, completed activities, ≥4.0/5 content), you don't pay for it.
